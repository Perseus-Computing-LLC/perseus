Inside the brief
What we build

Live context, Perseus Vault governed memory, Ledger evidence and provenance, and deployment inside the data boundary.

Why it matters

AI workflows can lose the state that made an answer useful. Perseus keeps current context, memory, and reviewable evidence with the workflow.

Measured evidence

LongMemEval-S: 82.0% (410/500) in the latest completed paired internal confirmation; matched full-context control 83.2% and delta -1.2 points. 99.2% retrieval recall@10, 13/13 BEAM correctness, and 40/s durable write at 100K entities remain separate measures, each labeled by method and condition.

Procurement posture

UEI PJS2LW7HAK35, CAGE 22JC5, SAM active, SPRS 110/110, CMMC Level 2 self-assessment evidence, approved DD2345/JCP status valid through 18 Aug 2031, MIT license, published SBOM, and NIST AI RMF-aligned architecture.

Where it fits
Cyber & Networks

AI/ML, DevSecOps, cyber, and enterprise IT workflows where context and evidence must stay inside the controlled network.

C3BM

Electronic Systems
